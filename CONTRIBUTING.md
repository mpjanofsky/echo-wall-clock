# Contributing

The most useful contribution is a report from different hardware or firmware. Please open an issue with:

- Clock firmware version (`firmwareVersion` in its `Discover.Response`), and the model on the back.
- Your Bluetooth adapter and OS/BlueZ version.
- What you sent, what the clock did, and the log lines (`logs/*.jsonl`), with the serial number,
  device token and Bluetooth addresses removed.

Open questions are listed in [PROTOCOL.md §7](PROTOCOL.md). Do not post Amazon's proprietary code or
firmware. Keep descriptions to what you observed on your own device.
